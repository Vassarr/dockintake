Step by step

Seven clicks. That's it.

Log into Squarespace

Head to squarespace.com and log in. Open the website with the contact form you want connected.

Open Settings

From your Squarespace dashboard, click Settings in the left-hand menu.

Find the Advanced section

Scroll down inside Settings until you see Advanced. Click to expand the menu.

Open Form & Pop-Up Storage

Inside the Advanced menu, click Form & Pop-Up Storage. This is where Squarespace lets you connect external services to your contact forms.

Connect Zapier

You'll see a list of services Squarespace can send form submissions to. Find Zapier and click Connect. A new window will open asking you to log in.

No Zapier account? Click Sign Up. Free plan works — we cover the paid features on our end.

Already have one? Log in. When prompted, click Allow or Authorize to give Zapier permission to receive form submissions from your Squarespace site.

What is Zapier? The middleware we use to listen for your form submissions and trigger your SMS alerts. Standard tool, widely trusted, you never have to interact with it again after this step.

Common questions

Just in case.

What if I'm on the Personal plan?
Two options: (1) Upgrade to Business — $23/month through Squarespace, gives you immediate access to this integration. (2) Let us build you a separate landing page hosted on our infrastructure. That page works independently of your existing site and hooks into the alert system directly. Mention which you'd prefer when you reply.
Will this slow down my Squarespace site?
No. The integration runs in the background after a form is submitted — your site speed and visitor experience are unchanged.
Does this work for multiple forms?
Yes. If you have several forms (Contact, Quote Request, etc.), tell us which ones to connect when you reply and we'll wire them all up.
Can I disconnect later?
Yes. Settings → Advanced → Form & Pop-Up Storage → click Zapier → Disconnect. No long-term contract — cancel anytime.
What if I change my phone number?
Email us. Takes 30 seconds to update on our end.
